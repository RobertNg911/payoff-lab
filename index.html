Game theory, simulated.

Payoff Lab is a YouTube channel that turns game theory into experiments you can watch.

In every video, hundreds of simple agents each follow one rule: fight, share, cheat, or cooperate. They compete for food, the winners have more offspring, and we watch which strategy takes over. Every video is a real simulation. The rules are shown on screen, the run we show is a typical one rather than a lucky one, and the result is checked against the published maths.

Latest video

8 Hawks, 72 Doves: Who Takes Over?

The Hawk-Dove game: aggressive hawks and peaceful doves share a world with limited food. Hawks win fights but pay for them in injuries. The simulation shows why aggression rises, then stops at a share set by the value of the food and the cost of a fight.

Payoff Lab Uploader

Payoff Lab Uploader is the small tool the channel's owner uses to publish these videos. It is a command-line program that runs on the owner's own computer and signs in with Google to use the YouTube Data API. It is not a public service: it has one user, the owner of the Payoff Lab channel.

What it does with the YouTube account it is signed in to:

  • reads the channel's ID and name, to refuse uploads to any channel other than Payoff Lab;
  • uploads a finished video as private, with its title, description, tags and chapters;
  • sets the video's thumbnail and uploads its English captions;
  • reads the video's processing and privacy status;
  • makes the video public, or schedules it, only when the owner runs the publish command.

It does not read, change or delete anything else on the account. How it handles Google user data is described in the Privacy Policy.
